UNDER THE HOOD

One interface. Two kinds of evidence.

All 15 material families are considered. The evidence catalogue currently covers sulfamethoxazole, amaranth and dissolved lead(II), with 16 observations from 11 papers. Missing evidence is not treated as failure.

Deterministic ranking

The decision policy uses 60% suitability, 25% setup compatibility and 15% recovery practicality. Unknown ratings stay as score ranges. The interface reports a shortlist when the ranges overlap. Two alternative weight sets are also checked.

Trained prediction model

The ML output is the dataset’s reported mlogk target. Its logarithm base is unverified, so it is not converted into a physical rate or removal percentage. It is available only for the unspecified dataset TiO₂ profile and supported conditions.

What this prototype does not establish

Evaluation is preliminary because study IDs are missing. Material grade, size, surface area and water matrix effects are not modelled. There is no independent laboratory validation or certification of treated water.
