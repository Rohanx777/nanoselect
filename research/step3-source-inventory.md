# Step 3: Research source inventory

Search date: 27 October 2026. Status: **screening leads for review, not yet curated ranking evidence**.

## Scope and method

The current project has 15 candidate families and three supported pollutants (SMX, amaranth and dissolved lead). Searches targeted uncovered family–pollutant combinations and exploratory arsenic and chromium sources. Searches used scientific article titles, candidate and pollutant names, and publisher, PubMed and open-access article pages. This is a focused search, not a systematic review or proof that a study does not exist.

The screening notes below reflect the title, abstract or sections available in the search results; every prospective record still needs its source, exact formulation, conditions, endpoint, units and access rights checked before entering the application. Direct full-text access was confirmed for the Fe(0)/chromium and graphene oxide/chromium articles; several other direct page opens returned a CAPTCHA, access denial or rate limit, so their notes rely on indexed search content and are not full-text reviews. Do not equate a composite with either of its component materials, an adsorption capacity with removal percentage, or an optimum with a general valid range. A material family shown in the leads column is a **possible research direction**, not a new supported catalogue pair.

## What remains unresolved

- The candidate leads above do not yet change the current 11-of-45 catalogue coverage. Full extraction and verification belong to Step 4.
- A lead may ultimately be excluded if its particle dimensions, contaminant species, formulation, conditions, outcome or access scope cannot be confirmed. Some search results concerned bulk films, different dyes, different antibiotics, soil, or composites and were not counted as standalone nanomaterial evidence.
- Search effort remains uneven across the 34 gaps. In particular, a suitable amaranth/CNT record, a standalone nano-alumina/lead record, and several silver and amaranth combinations were not confirmed by this pass. This is not an exhaustive claim of nonexistence.
- The user-provided generic size and surface-area ranges cannot fill missing experimental descriptors. The current TiO₂ model's study identifiers are still unavailable.
